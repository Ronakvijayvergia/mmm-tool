Key MMM Concepts Implemented

Adstock (Carryover)

Models how marketing effects persist over time. TV ad seen today influences purchases for weeks. Supports geometric decay and Weibull distributions for flexible decay patterns.

Geometric Weibull CDF Weibull PDF

Saturation (Diminishing Returns)

Captures how each additional dollar spent delivers less incremental value. The Hill function is the standard in Meta Robyn. Four curve types available.

Hill Power Logistic Michaelis-Menten

Budget Optimization

Uses the equimarginal principle: reallocate budget until marginal returns equalize across channels. Constrained optimization with SLSQP solver.

Equimarginal Marginal ROI Scenarios

Calibration

Anchor MMM outputs with ground truth from lift studies, geo-experiments, and A/B tests. Combines statistical modeling with empirical evidence.

Geo-Tests Lift Studies A/B Tests
